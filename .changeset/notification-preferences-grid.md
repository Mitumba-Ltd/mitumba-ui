New `NotificationPreferencesGrid`: notification settings as a category × channel grid. One switch per channel a category uses, a lock and hint on required categories, unavailable channels disabled with their value kept, a spinner on cells being saved, and skeleton rows while loading. Cards on phones; a table with sticky channel headers from 600px. Also exports `DEFAULT_NOTIFICATION_COLUMNS` and the types `NotificationPreferencesGridProps`, `NotificationPreferenceRow`, `NotificationPreferenceChange` and `NotificationChannel`.
